## Review and Maintenance

All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United Kingdom. Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the IT Services register. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies.

## Response Targets

Severity 1 incidents must be acknowledged within 10 minutes. Severity 2 incidents must be acknowledged within 60 minutes.

## Related Resources

## Post-incident Review

A written post-mortem must be published within 7 working days of resolving any Severity 1 incident.
