## Overview

This policy sets the rules for business expenses claimed by Northwind Analytics employees in United States.

## Limits

The daily meal allowance is USD 30. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to USD 150 per night.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any single expense above USD 15.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United States.

## Approvals and Deadlines

Claims above USD 1,000 need approval from the department head. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date.
