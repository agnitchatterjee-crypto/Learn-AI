## Response Targets

Severity 1 incidents must be acknowledged within 5 minutes. Severity 2 incidents must be acknowledged within 60 minutes.

## Review and Maintenance

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations.

## Post-incident Review

A written post-mortem must be published within 3 working days of resolving any Severity 1 incident.

## Scope and Applicability

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Singapore.

## Compliance and Exceptions

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.
