## First Week

New joiners in Germany receive their laptop within 5 working days of their start date.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.

## Support

Every new joiner is paired with an onboarding buddy for 4 weeks.

## Probation

The probation period lasts 6 months.

## Background

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. ## Roles and Responsibilities

Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.
