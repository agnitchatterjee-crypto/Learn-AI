## Overview

This policy sets the rules for business expenses claimed by Northwind Analytics employees in India.

## Limits

The daily meal allowance is INR 3,200. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to INR 10,000 per night.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Finance register. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Glossary and Definitions

All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in India. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Finance register. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any single expense above INR 1,200.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.

## Approvals and Deadlines

Claims above INR 200,000 need approval from the department head. Claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense date.
