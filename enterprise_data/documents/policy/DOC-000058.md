## Dependencies

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise.

## Booking

All travel must be booked at least 21 days in advance through the approved travel portal.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Class of Travel

Business class may be booked only for flights longer than 5 hours.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Questions about this document should be directed to the Finance team through the internal service desk. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.

## Per Diem

Employees based in United States receive a per diem of USD 50 on approved trips.
