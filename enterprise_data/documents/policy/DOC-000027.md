## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 1 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Dependencies

Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United States.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 11:00 and 17:00 local time.

## Home Office Stipend

A monthly home-office stipend of USD 50 is paid to eligible employees.
