## Glossary and Definitions

Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy.

## Competitive Quotes

Purchases above EUR 25,000 require at least 3 competitive quotes.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board.

## Legal Review

Any contract with a value above EUR 25,000 must be reviewed by Legal & Compliance before signature.
