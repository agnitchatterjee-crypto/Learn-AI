## First Week

New joiners in India receive their laptop within 5 working days of their start date.

## Reporting and Metrics

Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register. Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies.

## Support

Every new joiner is paired with an onboarding buddy for 12 weeks.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in India. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.

## Probation

The probation period lasts 6 months.
