## Booking

All travel must be booked at least 21 days in advance through the approved travel portal.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Singapore. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Finance register. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Class of Travel

Business class may be booked only for flights longer than 6 hours.

## Scope and Applicability

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies.

## Dependencies

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process.

## Per Diem

Employees based in Singapore receive a per diem of SGD 40 on approved trips.
