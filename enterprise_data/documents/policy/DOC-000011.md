## Competitive Quotes

Purchases above GBP 10,000 require at least 2 competitive quotes.

## Related Resources

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United Kingdom.

## Legal Review

Any contract with a value above GBP 100,000 must be reviewed by Legal & Compliance before signature.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.
