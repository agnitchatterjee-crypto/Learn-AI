## Purpose

This policy explains how Northwind Analytics employees in India accrue and use paid time off.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in India receive 16 days of paid time off per calendar year. In addition, employees receive 8 days of paid sick leave.

## Related Resources

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in India.

## Carryover

Unused paid time off may be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 6 days. Days above this cap expire on 31 March.

## Background

Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies.

## Requesting Leave

Leave of more than three consecutive days must be requested at least 7 working days in advance through the HR portal.
