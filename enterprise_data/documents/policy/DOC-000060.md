## Overview

This policy sets the rules for business expenses claimed by Northwind Analytics employees in Germany.

## Limits

The daily meal allowance is EUR 40. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to EUR 125 per night.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Questions about this document should be directed to the Finance team through the internal service desk. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Germany. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any single expense above EUR 20.

## Approvals and Deadlines

Claims above EUR 2,500 need approval from the department head. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date.
