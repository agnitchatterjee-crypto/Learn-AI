## Eligibility

Employees in United Kingdom whose role allows it may work remotely under this policy.

## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 1 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice.

## Background

All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United Kingdom. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk.

## Scope and Applicability

All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United Kingdom. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 09:00 and 15:00 local time.

## Home Office Stipend

A monthly home-office stipend of GBP 30 is paid to eligible employees.
