## Purpose

This policy explains how Northwind Analytics employees in United Kingdom accrue and use paid time off.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in United Kingdom receive 15 days of paid time off per calendar year. In addition, employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave.

## Reporting and Metrics

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy.

## Carryover

Unused paid time off may be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 6 days. Days above this cap expire on 31 March.

## Requesting Leave

Leave of more than three consecutive days must be requested at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal.
