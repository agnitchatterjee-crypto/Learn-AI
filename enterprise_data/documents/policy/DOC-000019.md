## Reviews

Every pull request requires approval from at least 2 reviewer(s) before merging.

## Quality Gates

Automated test coverage must remain at or above 85 percent. Pull requests should not exceed 500 changed lines.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Related Resources

Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Engineering register.
