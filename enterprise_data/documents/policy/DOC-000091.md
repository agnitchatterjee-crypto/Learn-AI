## Reporting and Metrics

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Questions about this document should be directed to the Finance team through the internal service desk. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Finance register.

## Booking

All travel must be booked at least 14 days in advance through the approved travel portal.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.

## Class of Travel

Business class may be booked only for flights longer than 4 hours.

## Background

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in India. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect.

## Per Diem

Employees based in India receive a per diem of INR 4,800 on approved trips.
