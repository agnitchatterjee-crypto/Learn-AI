## Competitive Quotes

Purchases above USD 5,000 require at least 3 competitive quotes.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Finance register. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United States.

## Review and Maintenance

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.

## Legal Review

Any contract with a value above USD 50,000 must be reviewed by Legal & Compliance before signature.

## Glossary and Definitions

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.
