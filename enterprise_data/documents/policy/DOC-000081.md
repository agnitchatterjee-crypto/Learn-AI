## Dependencies

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise.

## Annual Budget

Each employee in Singapore has an annual learning budget of SGD 1,500 and may spend up to 30 working hours on learning each quarter.

## Background

Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.

## Review and Maintenance

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process.
