## Retention Schedule

Contracts are retained for 5 years after expiry. Financial records are retained for 10 years. HR records are retained for 7 years after employment ends. Email is retained for 1 years.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Questions about this document should be directed to the Legal & Compliance team through the internal service desk. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.
