## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 16 characters long and must be changed every 90 days.

## Dependencies

All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in India. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Account Lockout

Accounts are locked after 3 consecutive failed sign-in attempts and can be unlocked through the IT service desk.

## Multi-factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all corporate systems accessed from India.

## Related Resources
