## Roles and Responsibilities

Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 10 characters long and must be changed every 180 days.

## Account Lockout

Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts and can be unlocked through the IT service desk.

## Related Resources
