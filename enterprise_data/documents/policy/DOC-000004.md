## Eligibility

Employees in Germany whose role allows it may work remotely under this policy.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Germany. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 5 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Dependencies

Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 11:00 and 15:00 local time.

## Home Office Stipend

A monthly home-office stipend of EUR 40 is paid to eligible employees.

## Reporting and Metrics

Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice.
