## Eligibility

Employees in United States whose role allows it may work remotely under this policy.

## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 11:00 and 15:00 local time.
