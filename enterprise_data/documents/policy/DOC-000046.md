## Dependencies

Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Response Targets

Severity 1 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. Severity 2 incidents must be acknowledged within 60 minutes.

## Post-incident Review

A written post-mortem must be published within 7 working days of resolving any Severity 1 incident.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process.

## Reporting and Metrics

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the IT Services register.
