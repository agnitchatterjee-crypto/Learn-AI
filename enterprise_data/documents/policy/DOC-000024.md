## Eligibility

Employees in Singapore whose role allows it may work remotely under this policy.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Singapore. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations.

## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 09:00 and 15:00 local time.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Singapore.

## Home Office Stipend

A monthly home-office stipend of SGD 40 is paid to eligible employees.
