## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 16 characters long and must be changed every 180 days.

## Account Lockout

Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts and can be unlocked through the IT service desk.

## Multi-factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all corporate systems accessed from United States.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the IT Services register.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect.
