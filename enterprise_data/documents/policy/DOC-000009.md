## Scope and Applicability

Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the IT Services register.

## Background

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.

## Response Targets

Severity 1 incidents must be acknowledged within 10 minutes. Severity 2 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.

## Post-incident Review

A written post-mortem must be published within 7 working days of resolving any Severity 1 incident.
