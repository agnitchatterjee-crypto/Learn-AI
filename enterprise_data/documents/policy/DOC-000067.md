## Overview

This policy sets the rules for business expenses claimed by Northwind Analytics employees in Singapore.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.

## Limits

The daily meal allowance is SGD 60. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to SGD 200 per night.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any single expense above SGD 20.

## Scope and Applicability

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise.

## Approvals and Deadlines

Claims above SGD 500 need approval from the department head. Claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense date.

## Glossary and Definitions

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.
