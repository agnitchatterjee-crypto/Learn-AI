## Roles and Responsibilities

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 10 characters long and must be changed every 180 days.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the IT Services register. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations.

## Related Resources

Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.

## Account Lockout

Accounts are locked after 3 consecutive failed sign-in attempts and can be unlocked through the IT service desk.

## Multi-factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all corporate systems accessed from Singapore.
