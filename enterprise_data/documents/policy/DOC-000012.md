## Roles and Responsibilities

All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United States. Questions about this document should be directed to the Legal & Compliance team through the internal service desk. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Retention Schedule

Contracts are retained for 10 years after expiry. Financial records are retained for 7 years. HR records are retained for 7 years after employment ends. Email is retained for 2 years.

## Reporting and Metrics

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.
