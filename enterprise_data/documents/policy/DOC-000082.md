## Reviews

Every pull request requires approval from at least 1 reviewer(s) before merging.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Quality Gates

Automated test coverage must remain at or above 80 percent. Pull requests should not exceed 500 changed lines.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Engineering register. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.
