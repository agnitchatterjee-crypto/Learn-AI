## Entitlement

Birth mothers in United States receive 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. Partners receive 8 weeks of fully paid paternity leave.

## Reporting and Metrics

Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.
