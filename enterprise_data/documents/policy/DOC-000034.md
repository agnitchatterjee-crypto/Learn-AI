## Eligibility

Employees in United Kingdom whose role allows it may work remotely under this policy.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise.

## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 5 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 09:00 and 16:00 local time.

## Review and Maintenance

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Home Office Stipend

A monthly home-office stipend of GBP 30 is paid to eligible employees.

## Glossary and Definitions

Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United Kingdom. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations.
