## Purpose

This policy explains how Northwind Analytics employees in Germany accrue and use paid time off.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in Germany receive 25 days of paid time off per calendar year. In addition, employees receive 7 days of paid sick leave.

## Scope and Applicability

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Carryover

Unused paid time off may be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 10 days. Days above this cap expire on 31 March.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.

## Requesting Leave

Leave of more than three consecutive days must be requested at least 10 working days in advance through the HR portal.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice.
