## Dependencies

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Legal & Compliance register. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.

## Retention Schedule

Contracts are retained for 10 years after expiry. Financial records are retained for 6 years. HR records are retained for 3 years after employment ends. Email is retained for 3 years.

## Reporting and Metrics

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Legal & Compliance register. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board.

## Scope and Applicability

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect.
