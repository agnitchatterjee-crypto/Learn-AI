## Dependencies

Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Competitive Quotes

Purchases above INR 80,000 require at least 2 competitive quotes.

## Scope and Applicability

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies.

## Legal Review

Any contract with a value above INR 4,000,000 must be reviewed by Legal & Compliance before signature.

## Glossary and Definitions

Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.
