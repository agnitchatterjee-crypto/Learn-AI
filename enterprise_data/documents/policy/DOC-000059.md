## Entitlement

Birth mothers in India receive 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. Partners receive 2 weeks of fully paid paternity leave.

## Review and Maintenance

Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register.

## Reporting and Metrics

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice.
