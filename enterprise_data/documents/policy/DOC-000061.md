## Overview

This policy sets the rules for business expenses claimed by Northwind Analytics employees in Germany.

## Glossary and Definitions

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations.

## Limits

The daily meal allowance is EUR 30. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to EUR 100 per night.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any single expense above EUR 20.

## Review and Maintenance

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Questions about this document should be directed to the Finance team through the internal service desk.

## Scope and Applicability

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Germany.

## Approvals and Deadlines

Claims above EUR 500 need approval from the department head. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
