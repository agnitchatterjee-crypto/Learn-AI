**Owner:** Anna Fischer
**Department:** Human Resources
**Status:** active
**Version:** 3
**Last updated:** 2025-07-18
**Region:** Germany

## Eligibility

Employees in Germany whose role allows it may work remotely under this policy.

## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 11:00 and 15:00 local time.

## Dependencies

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.

## Home Office Stipend

A monthly home-office stipend of EUR 50 is paid to eligible employees.
