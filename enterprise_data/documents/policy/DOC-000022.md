## Reviews

Every pull request requires approval from at least 3 reviewer(s) before merging.

## Quality Gates

Automated test coverage must remain at or above 70 percent. Pull requests should not exceed 500 changed lines.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Germany. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.
