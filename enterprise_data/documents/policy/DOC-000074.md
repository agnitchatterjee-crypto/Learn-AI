## Review and Maintenance

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Annual Budget

Each employee in Germany has an annual learning budget of EUR 2,000 and may spend up to 30 working hours on learning each quarter.

## Roles and Responsibilities

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.
