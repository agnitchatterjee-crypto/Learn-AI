## Glossary and Definitions

Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.

## Scope and Applicability

Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Finance register. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise.

## Booking

All travel must be booked at least 21 days in advance through the approved travel portal.

## Class of Travel

Business class may be booked only for flights longer than 6 hours.

## Per Diem

Employees based in Singapore receive a per diem of SGD 60 on approved trips.
