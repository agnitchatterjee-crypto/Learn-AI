## Eligibility

Employees in India whose role allows it may work remotely under this policy.

## Related Resources

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.

## Glossary and Definitions

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Background

Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 09:00 and 17:00 local time.

## Home Office Stipend

A monthly home-office stipend of INR 3,200 is paid to eligible employees.
