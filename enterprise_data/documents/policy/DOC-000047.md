## Compliance and Exceptions

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Germany. Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk.

## Response Targets

Severity 1 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. Severity 2 incidents must be acknowledged within 30 minutes.

## Dependencies

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the IT Services register. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.

## Post-incident Review

A written post-mortem must be published within 3 working days of resolving any Severity 1 incident.

## Scope and Applicability

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.
