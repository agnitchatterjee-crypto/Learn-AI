## Reporting and Metrics

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Germany.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must be changed every 90 days.

## Account Lockout

Accounts are locked after 10 consecutive failed sign-in attempts and can be unlocked through the IT service desk.

## Multi-factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all corporate systems accessed from Germany.

## Review and Maintenance

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.
