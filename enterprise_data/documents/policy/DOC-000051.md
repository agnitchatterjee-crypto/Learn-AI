## Roles and Responsibilities

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the IT Services register.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Response Targets

Severity 1 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. Severity 2 incidents must be acknowledged within 60 minutes.

## Dependencies

Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the IT Services register. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.

## Post-incident Review

A written post-mortem must be published within 7 working days of resolving any Severity 1 incident.
