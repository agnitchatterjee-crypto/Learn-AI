## Background

Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Singapore. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Singapore. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.

## Review and Maintenance

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy.

## Retention Schedule

Contracts are retained for 7 years after expiry. Financial records are retained for 10 years. HR records are retained for 5 years after employment ends. Email is retained for 2 years.
