## Review and Maintenance

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Retention Schedule

Contracts are retained for 7 years after expiry. Financial records are retained for 8 years. HR records are retained for 7 years after employment ends. Email is retained for 1 years.

## Scope and Applicability

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Legal & Compliance register.
