## Compliance and Exceptions

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Finance register. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Germany. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect.

## Competitive Quotes

Purchases above EUR 5,000 require at least 3 competitive quotes.
