## Overview

This policy sets the rules for business expenses claimed by Northwind Analytics employees in United Kingdom.

## Reporting and Metrics

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United Kingdom. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Compliance and Exceptions

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Questions about this document should be directed to the Finance team through the internal service desk.

## Limits

The daily meal allowance is GBP 50. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to GBP 100 per night.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any single expense above GBP 20.

## Approvals and Deadlines

Claims above GBP 2,500 need approval from the department head. Claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense date.
