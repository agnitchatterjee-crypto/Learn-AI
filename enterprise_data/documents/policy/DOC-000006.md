## Booking

All travel must be booked at least 21 days in advance through the approved travel portal.

## Background

Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Finance register. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.

## Class of Travel

Business class may be booked only for flights longer than 6 hours.

## Per Diem

Employees based in Germany receive a per diem of EUR 60 on approved trips.
