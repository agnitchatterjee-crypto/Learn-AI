## Dependencies

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in India.

## Annual Budget

Each employee in India has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 and may spend up to 40 working hours on learning each quarter.
