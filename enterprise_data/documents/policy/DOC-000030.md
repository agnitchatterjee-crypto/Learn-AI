## Purpose

This policy explains how Northwind Analytics employees in United Kingdom accrue and use paid time off.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in United Kingdom receive 15 days of paid time off per calendar year. In addition, employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave.

## Carryover

Unused paid time off may be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 9 days. Days above this cap expire on 31 March.

## Requesting Leave

Leave of more than three consecutive days must be requested at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal.
