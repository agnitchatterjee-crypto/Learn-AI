## First Week

New joiners in United Kingdom receive their laptop within 1 working days of their start date.

## Glossary and Definitions

Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United Kingdom. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process.

## Support

Every new joiner is paired with an onboarding buddy for 8 weeks.

## Probation

The probation period lasts 6 months.

## Dependencies

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.
