## Reviews

Every pull request requires approval from at least 1 reviewer(s) before merging.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Questions about this document should be directed to the Engineering team through the internal service desk.

## Related Resources

Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Germany.

## Quality Gates

Automated test coverage must remain at or above 75 percent. Pull requests should not exceed 800 changed lines.
