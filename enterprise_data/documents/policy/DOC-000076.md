## Review and Maintenance

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy.

## Background

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United States.

## Annual Budget

Each employee in United States has an annual learning budget of USD 1,000 and may spend up to 30 working hours on learning each quarter.
