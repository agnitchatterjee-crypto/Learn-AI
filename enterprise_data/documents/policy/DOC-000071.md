## First Week

New joiners in United States receive their laptop within 1 working days of their start date.

## Support

Every new joiner is paired with an onboarding buddy for 4 weeks.

## Reporting and Metrics

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice.

## Probation

The probation period lasts 6 months.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies.

## Scope and Applicability

All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United States. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register.
