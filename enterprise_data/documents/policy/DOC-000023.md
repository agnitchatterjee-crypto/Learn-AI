## Eligibility

Employees in Singapore whose role allows it may work remotely under this policy.

## Background

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Singapore. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.

## Review and Maintenance

Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.

## Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, agreed with their manager.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 09:00 and 16:00 local time.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time.

## Home Office Stipend

A monthly home-office stipend of SGD 40 is paid to eligible employees.
