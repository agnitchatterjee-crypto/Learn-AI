## Purpose

This policy explains how Northwind Analytics employees in Singapore accrue and use paid time off.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees in Singapore receive 28 days of paid time off per calendar year. In addition, employees receive 11 days of paid sick leave.

## Glossary and Definitions

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations.

## Carryover

Unused paid time off may be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 4 days. Days above this cap expire on 31 March.

## Requesting Leave

Leave of more than three consecutive days must be requested at least 14 working days in advance through the HR portal.

## Dependencies

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board.

## Scope and Applicability

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.
