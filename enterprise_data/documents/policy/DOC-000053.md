## Scope and Applicability

All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in Singapore. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.

## Reviews

Every pull request requires approval from at least 3 reviewer(s) before merging.

## Quality Gates

Automated test coverage must remain at or above 70 percent. Pull requests should not exceed 500 changed lines.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.
