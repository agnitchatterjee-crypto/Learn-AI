## Compliance and Exceptions

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Questions about this document should be directed to the Engineering team through the internal service desk. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.

## Reviews

Every pull request requires approval from at least 1 reviewer(s) before merging.

## Background

Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Engineering register. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice.

## Quality Gates

Automated test coverage must remain at or above 85 percent. Pull requests should not exceed 500 changed lines.
