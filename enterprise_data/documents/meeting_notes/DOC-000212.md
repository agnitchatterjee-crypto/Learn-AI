## Background

Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Data & Analytics register. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in all regions. Questions about this document should be directed to the Data & Analytics team through the internal service desk.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations.
