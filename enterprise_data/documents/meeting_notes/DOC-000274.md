## Compliance and Exceptions

Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Glossary and Definitions

Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Legal & Compliance register. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in all regions. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Questions about this document should be directed to the Legal & Compliance team through the internal service desk. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.
