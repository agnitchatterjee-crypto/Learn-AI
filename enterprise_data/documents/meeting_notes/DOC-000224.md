## Scope and Applicability

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in all regions. Questions about this document should be directed to the Finance team through the internal service desk. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.

## Dependencies

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process.
