## Review and Maintenance

Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk.

## Background

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.
