## Q: Which VPN client should I use?

Use SecureLink, available from the self-service software portal.

## Q: How do I reach the IT helpdesk?

Dial extension 2970 from any office phone or raise a ticket through the service desk.

## Q: How often are laptops replaced?

Laptops are refreshed every 4 years.
