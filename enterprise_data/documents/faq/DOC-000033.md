## Background

## Q: How do I reach the IT helpdesk?

Dial extension 5965 from any office phone or raise a ticket through the service desk.

## Q: How often are laptops replaced?

Laptops are refreshed every 4 years.

## Reporting and Metrics

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the IT Services register. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.
