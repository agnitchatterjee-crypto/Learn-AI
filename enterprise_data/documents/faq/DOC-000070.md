## Q: When are payslips released?

Payslips are released on day 25 of each month.

## Review and Maintenance

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise.

## Q: How many public holidays are there?

Employees in India get 9 public holidays per year.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in India.

## Scope and Applicability

Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.
