## Glossary and Definitions

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Q: When are payslips released?

Payslips are released on day 30 of each month.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register.
