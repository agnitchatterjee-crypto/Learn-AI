## Q: When are payslips released?

Payslips are released on day 30 of each month.

## Dependencies

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Questions about this document should be directed to the Human Resources team through the internal service desk.

## Q: How many public holidays are there?

Employees in United Kingdom get 11 public holidays per year.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform.

## Review and Maintenance

All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in United Kingdom. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Human Resources register.
