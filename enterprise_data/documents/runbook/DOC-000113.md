## Alert

This runbook covers the 'Queue backlog' alert for reporting-service. Trigger threshold: 10 (see monitoring dashboard).

## Scope and Applicability

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice.

## Triage

1. Check the dashboard for reporting-service. 2. Review recent deployments. 3. Post updates in #oncall-reporting-service.

## Mitigation

If the alert persists, restart the service with: kubectl rollout restart deployment/reporting-service
