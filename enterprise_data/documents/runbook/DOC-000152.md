## Alert

This runbook covers the 'High error rate' alert for payments-api. Trigger threshold: 80 (see monitoring dashboard).

## Triage

1. Check the dashboard for payments-api. 2. Review recent deployments. 3. Post updates in #oncall-payments-api.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Engineering register. Questions about this document should be directed to the Engineering team through the internal service desk. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy.

## Mitigation

If the alert persists, restart the service with: kubectl rollout restart deployment/payments-api

This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. ## Escalation

If the 'High error rate' alert is not resolved within 10 minutes, page the secondary on-call engineer.
