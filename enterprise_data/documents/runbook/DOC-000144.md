## Alert

This runbook covers the 'High error rate' alert for reporting-service. Trigger threshold: 2 (see monitoring dashboard).

## Triage

1. Check the dashboard for reporting-service. 2. Review recent deployments. 3. Post updates in #oncall-reporting-service.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise.

## Mitigation

If the alert persists, restart the service with: kubectl rollout restart deployment/reporting-service

## Escalation

If the 'High error rate' alert is not resolved within 15 minutes, page the secondary on-call engineer.
