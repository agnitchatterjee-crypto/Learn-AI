## Alert

This runbook covers the 'Elevated latency' alert for profile-sync. Trigger threshold: 10 (see monitoring dashboard).

## Triage

1. Check the dashboard for profile-sync. 2. Review recent deployments. 3. Post updates in #oncall-profile-sync.

## Mitigation

If the alert persists, restart the service with: kubectl rollout restart deployment/profile-sync

## Escalation

If the 'Elevated latency' alert is not resolved within 15 minutes, page the secondary on-call engineer.
