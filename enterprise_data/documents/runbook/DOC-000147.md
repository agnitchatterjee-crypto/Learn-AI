## Alert

This runbook covers the 'High error rate' alert for ledger-service. Trigger threshold: 5 (see monitoring dashboard).

## Triage

1. Check the dashboard for ledger-service. 2. Review recent deployments. 3. Post updates in #oncall-ledger-service.

## Background

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Engineering register. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review.

## Mitigation

If the alert persists, restart the service with: kubectl rollout restart deployment/ledger-service

## Escalation

If the 'High error rate' alert is not resolved within 5 minutes, page the secondary on-call engineer.
