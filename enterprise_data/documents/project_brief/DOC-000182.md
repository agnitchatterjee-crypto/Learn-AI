## Goal

Project Umbra aims to improve efficiency in Human Resources and reduce operating cost.

## Background

Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in all regions.

## Timeline

The project starts on 2 November 2023 and the target launch date is 2 July 2024.
