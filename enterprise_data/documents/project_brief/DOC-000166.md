## Goal

Project Xenon aims to improve efficiency in IT Services and reduce operating cost.

## Background

Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.

## Scope and Applicability

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Questions about this document should be directed to the IT Services team through the internal service desk. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements.

## Timeline

The project starts on 9 October 2023 and the target launch date is 9 January 2024.

## Budget

## Stakeholders

## Risks

Key risks include resourcing constraints, vendor delays and changing requirements.

## Dependencies

Northwind Analytics values transparency, so material changes are announced on the intranet before they take effect. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in all regions. Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review.
