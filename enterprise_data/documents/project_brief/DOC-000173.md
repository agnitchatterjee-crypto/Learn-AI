## Background

Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised. Records related to this process are stored in the approved system of record and retained according to the data retention policy. Audit and compliance teams may request evidence of adherence at any time. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.

## Goal

Project Ibis aims to improve efficiency in Sales and reduce operating cost.

## Reporting and Metrics

Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Feedback and suggested improvements are welcome and are triaged at the monthly process review. Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations.

## Glossary and Definitions

Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams have read and understood these requirements. Metrics for this process are reported quarterly to the leadership team as part of the operating review. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Sales register. This document is reviewed annually and whenever there is a material change to the underlying process.

## Timeline
