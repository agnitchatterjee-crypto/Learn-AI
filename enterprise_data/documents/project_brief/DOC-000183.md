## Goal

Project Tundra aims to improve efficiency in Customer Support and reduce operating cost.

## Reporting and Metrics

Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. All employees are expected to follow this guidance in addition to applicable local laws in all regions. Teams should document any workaround they adopt so that it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, standardised.

## Timeline

The project starts on 12 May 2024 and the target launch date is 18 December 2024.

## Frequently Raised Concerns

Personal data handled as part of this process must be processed in line with the privacy notice. Third-party vendors who support this process must meet the same standards through contractual obligations. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Escalations that cannot be resolved at team level are raised to the relevant steering committee.

## Budget

## Stakeholders

## Scope and Applicability

Cross-functional dependencies should be identified early and tracked in the shared planning board. Contractors and temporary staff are covered unless their agreement states otherwise. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the department head and recorded in the Customer Support register. Training materials supporting this guidance are available on the learning platform. Where this document conflicts with local regulation, the stricter requirement applies.

## Risks

Key risks include resourcing constraints, vendor delays and changing requirements.
